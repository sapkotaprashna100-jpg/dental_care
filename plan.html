Project Plan – 100SMILES Dental Care

Prepared by: Prashna Sapkota  |  Date: 11 November 2025

Goal

The purpose of this project is to create a clean and welcoming website for 100SMILES Dental Care that helps potential patients understand the clinic, explore services easily, and contact the practice without confusion. My main measurable goal is to raise online appointment enquiries by around 20% in the first month. Another goal is to reduce repeated questions about the clinic’s address and opening hours by at least 30% by highlighting these details clearly across the website.

Success Evaluation

I will evaluate the success of the site using straightforward, realistic checks:

  • Weekly enquiry tracking: Count how many email enquiries and phone calls come directly from the information provided on the website.
  • Promotional bookings: Monitor how many patients respond to the “20% Off First Appointment” offer via email.
  • Ease of finding details: Ask two classmates to locate the clinic’s address and opening hours from the home page in under 10 seconds.

Target Audience

Main audience: Residents in the Razole area who want a dependable, family-friendly dental practice. This includes parents, young adults needing affordable dental care, and older adults seeking trusted long-term treatment.

Design decisions to suit the audience:

  • Essential contact details shown clearly: Phone, email, and address available on every page for quick access.
  • Easy-to-read content: Short paragraphs, bullet points, and simple explanations suitable for all age groups.
  • Clear services overview: Quick summaries of check-ups, cleanings, cosmetic services, implants, orthodontics, and emergency care.
  • Trust-building elements: Experience, clinic history, and staff photos to create a personal and safe impression.
  • Responsive design: Layout optimised for mobile users, ensuring easy tapping and reading.

Site Flowchart

The website will follow a simple structure with one main Home page that leads visitors to four major sections. The flowchart below outlines how each page connects and guides the user through the information.

Pages Included

  • Home (index.html): Brief introduction, promotional offers, quick services, contact details, and opening hours.
  • About Us (about.html): Breakdown of dental treatments with short explanations of each service.
  • Services (services.html): Friendly profiles and photos of all staff members.
  • Articles (articles.html): Informative dental articles with summaries and links for further reading.
  • Contact (contact.html): Address, phone, email, hours, and instructions for booking appointments.

Content Scope (from client)

  • Clinic background and mission, emphasising long-term service to the community.
  • Full list of dental services: check-ups, cleans, implants, orthodontics, cosmetic treatments, and emergency care.
  • Team members: Dr. Johanna Grey, Dr. Benedict Sloan, hygienist Richard Attam, and receptionist April Kepner.

I will adjust the client’s text for clarity, break long paragraphs, and make the content more suitable for web reading.

Design & Build Notes

  • Navigation: Use a fixed structure with consistent header and footer across all five pages.
  • Images: Only use the provided images, ensure correct alt text, and compress them for faster loading.
  • CSS: Build one linked stylesheet with readable colours, simple layout, and consistent spacing.
  • Validation: Ensure all HTML and CSS files pass validation checks.
  • Submission: Submit a ZIP file containing the full site, all images, and plan.html.
