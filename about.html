Our Story

Located at 33 Fantail Street, Razole, Queensland, 100SMILES Dental Care has proudly served the community for more than 30 years. With over 50 years of combined professional dental experience, we are committed to providing friendly, reliable, and compassionate oral care for patients of all ages.

Our services range from routine wellness check-ups and cleanings to orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, emergency treatment, diagnostics, and patient education. We ensure every patient receives comprehensive care and the knowledge needed to make informed dental-health decisions.

Our New Dental Technologies

  • Digital X-Rays: High-resolution images with reduced radiation exposure.
  • Intraoral Cameras: Detailed imaging for accurate detection and diagnosis.
  • Laser Dentistry: Minimally invasive treatments with less pain and faster healing.
  • CAD/CAM Technology: Same-day crowns, veneers, and restorations made on-site.
  • 3D Printing: Precision dental models, appliances, and surgical guides.
  • Teledentistry: Virtual consultations available for convenient care.
