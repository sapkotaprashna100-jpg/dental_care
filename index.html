Our Story

Located at 33 Fantail Street, Razole, 100SMILES Dental Care has proudly served the community for over 30 years. With more than 50 years of combined dental expertise, our team delivers friendly, reliable, and compassionate dental care for patients of all ages.

We offer a full full spectrum of dental services — from general check-ups and cleanings to implants, orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry, diagnostics, and emergency care. Our priority is not only treatment, but also patient education, helping you make informed decisions about your dental health.

Our New Dental Technologies

  • Digital X-Rays: High-resolution imaging with lower radiation and faster diagnosis.
  • Intraoral Cameras: Detailed visual assessments for early detection.
  • Laser Dentistry: Minimally invasive procedures with reduced pain and quicker healing.
  • CAD/CAM Technology: Same-day crowns, bridges, and restorations with perfect precision.
  • 3D Printing: Accurate dental models, appliances, and surgical guides.
  • Teledentistry: Virtual consultations for convenience and accessibility.
